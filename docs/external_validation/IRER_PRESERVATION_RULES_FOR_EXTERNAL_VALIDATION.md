# IRER Preservation Rules for External Validation

Status: provisional until Claude review.

1. IRER formulations remain primary.
2. External models are comparison instruments, not replacements.
3. A match to NLS, Q-ball, CGLE, analogue-fluid, or related language does not erase IRER terminology, authorship, or project framing.
4. External validation asks whether measured Quantule Mapper dynamics behave as expected under known laws.
5. A failed external comparison narrows the mapping; it does not automatically falsify all IRER.
6. A passing external comparison validates the simulator's faithfulness to that known law or behavioural expectation, not physical reality.
7. No result may be promoted to matter, gravity, or unification without separate tests.

This document exists to prevent the project from drifting into existing-model identity merely because those models are convenient comparison targets.
