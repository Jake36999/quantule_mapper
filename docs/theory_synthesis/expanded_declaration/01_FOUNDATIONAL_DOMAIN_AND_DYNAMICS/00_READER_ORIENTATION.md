# Reader Orientation

Status: transcript-replacement archive for the AIS/PIF -> Informational Indifference -> gradients -> Axis of Least Effort -> Informational Parallels -> FMIA family.

This folder is not a summary, template, or evidence index. It is intended to preserve the intellectual content needed to reconstruct this concept family if the raw transcripts, v9 declaration file, and Phase 1 ledger are unavailable.

## Preservation Standard

The archive preserves relevant formulations with enough surrounding dialogue to recover:

- the motivating question;
- Jake's complete wording and reasoning;
- the AI response or formal translation;
- Jake's subsequent response;
- continued development;
- alternatives and contradictions;
- later revision and present status.

A short quote, record ID, source anchor, or paraphrase is not sufficient. Editorial notes are allowed only after the preserved material. They must not replace it.

When unrelated personal material is removed, the archive uses this exact marker:

`[Unrelated personal material omitted; no IRER reasoning removed.]`

No ellipsis is used where conceptually relevant reasoning has been removed.

## Concept-Family Boundary

Included core concepts:

- AIS: A-temporal Informational Substrate.
- PIF: Primordial/Potential Information Field, with unresolved relation to AIS.
- Informational Indifference.
- Potentiality Indifference.
- informational gradients.
- Gradient-Derived Informational Forces where they arise from this family.
- Axis of Least Effort.
- path of least activity.
- probability parallels / informational parralels / Informational Parallels.
- FMIA: Fields of Minimal Informational Action.

Included bridge material:

- Payan States, spin-along-axis, and coupled rotational information indifferences where they explain the origin of least-effort pathing.
- quantule chiral pairs and missing angles where they extend path selection into geometry.
- simulation and implementation status where it changes what FMIA currently means.

Deferred material:

- OIW, RD, PAS, RFD, Quantules, Payan States, Angular Deficits, observer, consciousness, time, gravity, materials, and Aletheia are preserved only when needed to understand this family. Their full treatment belongs to later family archives.

## Historical Layer Labels

The archive uses these labels:

- `Original v9 formulation`: statement preserved from the clean v9 declaration.
- `Transcript-recovered formulation`: Jake wording recovered from transcript material.
- `AI formal translation`: AI translation, equation, analogy, or polished theory insert.
- `Author response`: Jake's explicit response after AI output.
- `Later incorporated`: later Jake-authored or declaration-level use shows the idea entered the theory.
- `Adjacent/deferred`: relevant context but destined for another concept family.
- `Post-v9 revision`: later correction or status narrowing.
- `Superseded implementation`: old implementation or proxy no longer current.
- `Current open hypothesis`: still unresolved.

## Formal Status Labels

Equations, variables, and technical mechanisms are marked as:

- `IRER-native definition`.
- `Proposed IRER formalisation`.
- `Standard-formalism analogy`.
- `Implementation proxy`.
- `Measured observable`.
- `Superseded implementation`.

## Adoption Status Labels

AI outputs use only:

- `explicitly_accepted`.
- `accepted_with_modification`.
- `later_incorporated`.
- `exploratory_only`.
- `explicitly_rejected`.
- `not_evidenced`.
- `unclear`.

Silence, politeness, or continued conversation is not acceptance.
