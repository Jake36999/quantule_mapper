> Original Insight / Distinction (User-Provided, revised and significantly expanded for Appendix A): My original concept, often phrased by me as systems following an "axis of least effort" or seeking "potentiality indifference," posits a fundamental drive within IRER towards configurations and trajectories that are the most informationally economical or maximally coherent with the local field. > Key aspects and implications I have developed for FMIA include:

FMIA defines the default stabilization channel, minimizing Entropy as Resonance Fragmentation, and may act as informational low-pass filters suppressing novelty. Local FMIA breakdown in field-chaotic zones and subsequent informational re-seeding for informational phase transitions are part of ongoing thought on FMIA violation conditions.

Informational Parallels may bifurcate, converge (forming Inter-FMIA Tension Zones or Informational Caustics), or exhibit Informational Path Curvature & Torsion. Overlapping FMIA trajectories may form Interference Channels. I am also exploring Informational Vorticity (helical formations). They can terminate or deflect at Angular Deficits or Chorotic Boundaries.

> 6. Quantized Paths, Geodesics, Waveguides, Superhighways, Metric Tensor, and FMIA-Payan State Coupling. In structured manifolds, FMIA trajectories may appear quantized as informational geodesics/waveguides. Multiple paths might merge into Informational Superhighways. Coherence-locking of Payan States across aligned Quantules may reinforce FMIA stability, forming spin-locked conduits, while misaligned clusters could deflect FMIA flow.

>
> 8. FMIA Path Hysteresis & Topological Memory. FMIA paths may exhibit hysteresis effects and topological memory.

Potentiality Indifference describes the state sought along FMIA paths, potentially linking to an informational Equivalence Principle.
