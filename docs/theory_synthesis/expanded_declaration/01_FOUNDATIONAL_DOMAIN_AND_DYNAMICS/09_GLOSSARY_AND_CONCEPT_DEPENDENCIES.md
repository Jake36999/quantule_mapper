# Glossary And Concept Dependencies

Status: self-contained glossary for a collaborator reading this family without other IRER documents.

## Core Terms

**AIS: A-temporal Informational Substrate**  
The v9 foundational domain: a pre-spacetime, generative field/domain of informational potential. In this archive it is an IRER-native ontology claim, not an implementation grid.

**PIF: Primordial/Potential Information Field**  
Later/parallel term used in AI recaps, IQG framing, and implementation-adjacent contexts. Its relation to AIS is unresolved.

**Informational Indifference**  
The proposed tendency of the informational domain toward an equilibrium-like or unresolved state. Deviations from this baseline create gradients.

**Potentiality Indifference**  
Jake's May 18 alternate/refining phrase for Informational Indifference. It may emphasize unresolved potentiality before locality or gradient selection. Status unresolved.

**Informational gradient**  
A directional difference in informational potential, resonance, entropy, phase, density, readiness, topology, or coherence. In IRER it is proposed to drive dynamics.

**Axis of Least Effort**  
Jake's phrase for the direction or path by which an informational system can preserve coherence or reduce effort/tension. Direct precursor to FMIA.

**Path of Least Activity**  
Jake's May 17 phrase. Likely an early form of Axis of Least Effort/FMIA, but exact synonymy is unresolved.

**Probability parallel / parralel of probabilities**  
Jake's May 17 phrase for a stable path across probability-like manifold structure that reduces entropy and delocalisation. Later folded toward Informational Parallels.

**Informational Parallel**  
Coherent path/channel/manifoldic line through the informational domain. Later v9 also uses "Coherent Manifold Channels."

**FMIA: Fields of Minimal Informational Action**  
Mature v9 principle that systems tend to evolve along paths minimizing informational action or effort. Built from Jake's least-activity/least-effort/parallel language and AI-assisted formal translation.

## Adjacent Terms Needed Here

**OIW: Ontological Informational Wave**  
Wave-like informational excitation in AIS/PIF. Needed because gradients and FMIA paths shape OIW propagation.

**RD: Resonance Density**  
Measure of local coherence/intensity of resonance. Used in later implementation and FMIA placeholder functionals.

**PAS: Potential Actualization State / Phase Alignment Score depending context**  
In v9, PAS often means Potential Actualization State. In some AI replies, PAS appears as Phase Alignment Score. This ambiguity must be tracked. PAS is relevant because coherence thresholds and collapse readiness affect pathing.

**RFD: Resolution Field Dynamics**  
IRER collapse/resolution dynamics: the process by which potentials become stable structures or novelty. FMIA paths may guide or be conditioned by RFD.

**Quantule**  
Localized stable informational structure. Quantules may follow FMIA paths and carry Payan-like internal modes.

**Payan State**  
Discrete rotational/internal resonance mode of a Quantule. Relevant here because Payan spin-axis alignment appears in Jake's May 18 least-effort sentence and AI torque mapping.

**Angular Deficit / Missing Angle**  
A mismatch or non-closure in informational/manifold geometry. Jake asks whether missing angles can be calculated to find stable structures.

**Informational Manifold Topology**  
The proposed topology/geometry of AIS/PIF. Informational Parallels, manifold angles, angular deficits, and FMIA channels depend on it.

**S-NCGL: Sourced Non-local Complex Ginzburg-Landau**  
Later computational engine/analogue. Current status: useful implementation proxy pending first-principles derivation from AIS/Informational Indifference.

**FMIA Stable Proxy**  
Historical FMIA-labelled implementation proxy reported as obsolete in December 2025. Not current engine unless code artifacts prove otherwise.

## Common Pitfalls

- Do not treat AIS and PIF as resolved synonyms.
- Do not treat "desire" or "goal" as literal physics unless Jake approves that reading.
- Do not treat AI equations as Jake-originated theory.
- Do not treat FMIA Stable Proxy as current implementation.
- Do not treat simulation analogues as proof of ontology.
- Do not promote candidate terms such as Informational Caustics or Vorticity without review.
