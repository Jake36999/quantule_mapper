## Review Notes

No candidate above should be treated as a numbered IRER concept until Jake approves its status. Several are likely sub-mechanisms of FMIA or Informational Manifold Topology rather than standalone concepts. Some may be metaphors that help preserve Jake's historical wording but should not enter formal theory language without definition.

The highest-priority decisions for this family are:

- whether Potentiality Indifference is distinct from Informational Indifference;
- whether Coupled Rotational Information Indifferences belongs under Payan States, FMIA, or both;
- whether Informational Caustics and Informational Vorticity should remain speculative path-topology addenda;
- whether Informational Propagators are a genuine force-mediation mechanism or just provisional language;
- whether localized FMIA in crystals belongs to a later applications chapter rather than foundational dynamics.
