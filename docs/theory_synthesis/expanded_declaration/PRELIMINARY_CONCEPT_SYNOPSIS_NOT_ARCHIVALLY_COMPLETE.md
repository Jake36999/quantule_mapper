## Concept: A-temporal Informational Substrate (AIS)

### Definition In IRER Terminology

`Original v9 formulation` `Formal status: IRER-native definition`

AIS is the proposed pre-spacetime, ontologically real field of informational potential from which all structure emerges. It is not merely an empty container, a passive backdrop, or a simulation grid. In IRER language, AIS is the domain in which potential configurations, resonant waveforms, gradients, and later actualized structures become possible before ordinary time, causality, and material locality are fully meaningful.

The key v9 addition is that AIS contains varying informational potentials. Those variations are tied to Informational Indifference: deviations from a baseline of indifference or equilibrium create gradients, and those gradients become the drivers of later dynamics. AIS therefore functions not as "space before space" in a simple geometric sense, but as the proposed condition under which geometry, persistence, interaction, and distinguishable structure can arise.

### Original Insight And Distinction

The original distinction in v9 is that AIS is generative rather than passive. A conventional vacuum or background field can be described inside an already formalized physics; AIS is proposed as the deeper IRER domain out of which the terms of ordinary physical description emerge.

The distinction matters because the later concepts in this chapter do not operate on already-existing particles moving through pre-existing space. Informational Indifference, gradients, Axis of Least Effort, Informational Parallels, and FMIA are all framed as internal tendencies or path-structures of this substrate/process domain.

### Development Of The Idea

`Original v9 formulation`: v9 states that Jake developed AIS during the early IRER conceptual phase, circa 2022-2024, before substantive AI engagement.

`Transcript-recovered elaboration`: Phase 1B did not locate the pre-2025 notes named in v9, but it did locate May 17-18 2025 passages where the surrounding vocabulary is Jake-authored before AI formal translation. The May 18 passage is especially important because Jake supplies Informational/Potentiality Indifference, Informational Parallels, Axis of Least Effort, and equilibrium reasoning in a single prompt before the AI response.

`Post-v9 conceptual revision`: later summaries and current theory synthesis often pair AIS with PIF. Phase 1B does not resolve whether PIF is a synonym, replacement, process-domain refinement, or later implementation-facing term.

### Internal Mechanism

AIS is proposed to operate by containing possible informational configurations whose differences create gradients. A stable configuration is not simply selected by an external rule; it emerges when local relations reduce unresolved tension, sustain resonance, or traverse lower-effort informational pathways.

Inputs or preconditions include informational potential, possible wave-like modulations, local differences in potential, and conditions for resonance. The governing tendency is Informational Indifference: the field tends toward unresolved or equilibrium-like neutrality unless a difference, resonance opportunity, or entropic potential creates a reason for locality to change. The state change is the emergence of gradients, then coherent wave/resonance structures, then actualization pathways. Failure conditions include insufficient gradient, incoherent resonance, excessive novelty/fragmentation, or path breakdown.

This is an editorial synthesis of v9 and Phase 1B. It is not a completed derivation.

### Relationship To Other IRER Concepts

AIS is upstream of OIWs, Informational Resonance, RD, PAS, RFD, Quantules, Payan States, Informational Manifold Topology, and FMIA. In this chapter, its direct relationships are:

- Informational Indifference supplies the substrate tendency.
- Informational gradients arise from deviations from that tendency.

- FMIA names the mature minimal-informational-action principle built from those path structures.

## Concept: Informational Indifference

### Definition In IRER Terminology

`Original v9 formulation` `Formal status: IRER-native definition`

Informational Indifference is the proposed tendency of the informational domain toward an equilibrium-like or unresolved-potential state. It is not a conventional law imposed from outside the substrate. In v9, it is the field's inherent tendency whose deviations generate informational gradients. Those gradients are then proposed to drive resonance, actualization, path selection, and emergent force-like behaviour.

### Original Insight And Distinction

The distinctive move is that IRER does not begin with fixed physical laws and then ask how information behaves inside them. It begins with a proposed informational tendency and asks how stable laws, paths, structures, and forces might emerge from deviations from that tendency.

The phrase "indifference" should not be flattened into mere apathy or absence. In context, it describes a baseline of unresolved potentiality or equilibrium. The field does not "choose" in a psychological sense. Teleological vocabulary appears historically, but current physics-facing language should translate it as tendency, relaxation, minimization, or unresolved potential unless Jake explicitly intends a stronger metaphysical claim.

### Development Of The Idea

`Original v9 formulation`: v9 defines Informational Indifference as the source of gradients in AIS.

`Transcript-recovered elaboration`: the pivotal May 18 Jake passage states that "Informational indifference" might be better described as "potentiality indifference" and connects it with equilibrium, informational parallels, angles of the manifold, locality change, resonance, and entropic potential. Phase 1B verifies this Jake-authored passage appears before the AI phase-field translation.

`Post-v9 conceptual revision`: later formalism-gap material cautions that importing phase-field or S-NCGL equations does not by itself derive the equation from Informational Indifference.

### Internal Mechanism

Informational Indifference supplies the baseline tendency. The field remains in an unresolved or equilibrium-like state unless conditions create a difference. A difference may appear as resonance opportunity, entropic potential, angular mismatch, phase gradient, density gradient, or path tension. That difference produces an informational gradient. The gradient supplies directionality: systems tend to move, resolve, stabilize, or reconfigure along pathways that reduce unresolved tension or increase coherent resonance.

This mechanism is documented as IRER reasoning, but its mathematical derivation remains open.

### Original Insight And Distinction

## Concept: Fields Of Minimal Informational Action (FMIA)

### Definition In IRER Terminology

`Original v9 formulation` `Formal status: IRER-native definition`

FMIA names the principle that informational systems tend to evolve, move, or configure along pathways within AIS/PIF that minimize informational action or effort. These pathways are Informational Parallels, coherent manifold channels, or manifoldic lines characterized by low resistance, low dissipation, or minimal informational tension.

FMIA should be read as a mature concept built from Jake's earlier Axis of Least Effort, path-of-least-activity, probability/informational-parallel, and potentiality-indifference language.

### Original Insight And Distinction

FMIA is analogous to least action, but the IRER claim is not merely that physical systems obey a known least-action principle. The claim is that an informational substrate has its own path-selection tendency, and ordinary least-action-like behaviour may be a downstream formal analogue of that deeper tendency.

### Development Of The Idea

`Transcript-recovered elaboration`: May 17 preserves "path of least activity" and probability parallels. May 18 preserves "axis of least effort" and "informational parralel." Jake also asks whether phase-field methods could be useful, initiating the standard-formalism translation.

`AI-assisted formal translation`: AI maps the idea to phase-field order parameters, free-energy functionals, least informational action, coherent parallel manifolds, geodesics, gradient forces, entropy forces, and spin torque.

`Original v9 formulation`: v9 names FMIA as the mature principle, states that AI helped formalize the name, and expands FMIA into a large mechanism family: action-like cost, Lagrangian analogy, path integral substrate, inertia/mass, path emergence/dissolution/saturation, entropy/novelty filtering, bifurcation, curvature, torsion, vorticity, caustics, observer navigation, quantized paths, waveguides, superhighways, Payan coupling, force mediation, hysteresis, inter-world synchronization, holographic boundary signatures, and equivalence-principle analogy.

### Internal Mechanism

FMIA has the following proposed mechanism:

1. A field region contains unresolved informational difference.
2. Informational gradients create directionality.

6. Paths may fail, dissolve, saturate, bifurcate, curl, terminate, or reseed novelty when gradients flatten, topology changes, or coherence breaks.

This mechanism is IRER-native as a conceptual principle. Its mathematical action functional remains a proposed formalisation.
