# Cross-Map: Recovered IRER Concepts × Current Results — Missed Connections

**Purpose.** Now that the original theory conversations are re-extracted in depth (May 2025), this file cross-analyses
the **recovered concepts** against the **current computational-stack results** (`docs/IRER_MASTER_HYPOTHESIS_CATALOG.md`)
to surface connections the main workflow did not make — where an early theoretical idea predicts, explains, or reframes
a later measured result. The template is Jake's own example: the May-17-2025 manifold **load-capacity / saturation**
theorising vs the 2026 gravity **saturation-cliff** finding.

**What this is / is not.** This is *interpretive cross-mapping to guide the next workflow steps*, not new claims and
not a revision of any verdict. Every result cited keeps its catalog verdict and the project's standing posture:
**no matter claim, no gravity claim, no emergent-physics claim.** A "missed connection" here means *a hypothesis worth
testing or a reframe worth considering*, not a confirmation. Concept sources are the re-extracted dossiers under
`conversations/2025-05/` and `declaration_v10/DELTA_REPORT_V9_TO_V10.md`; result sources are the hypothesis catalog
(§ refs below).

Confidence tags: **[strong]** the mapping is direct and specific · **[moderate]** structural but needs a test to confirm ·
**[speculative]** suggestive, flagged for judgement.

**Labeling discipline (2026-07-17 correction, Jake + Claude).** The MC sections below occasionally read too definitely
("the failures *are* IRER's predicted behaviour") and risk overfitting. Read them instead as: **an observed behaviour or
failure point is a *candidate* predicted mode, not a confirmed prediction.** The value is diagnostic — *does the recovered
theory suggest a way to avoid the failure, or a way to develop the system past it?* A match is a hypothesis-to-test and a
possible path forward, never a confirmation, and never an all-purpose excuse to relabel a null as a success. Each mapping
stands only as long as it generates a falsifiable test that could still fail.

## MC-1 — The gravity "failures" are IRER's *predicted* force behaviour, not failures to be Newtonian  [strong]

**Recovered concept.** Jake's Concept 20 (Gradient-Derived Informational Forces) predicts, in his own words, that
gravity-analogue forces are:
- **gradient-derived** — "Gravity-like effects: may emerge from **cumulative RD gradients**" (DoA conv. L7179);
- **saturating / rigid** — "Could a high-RD region **'cap out' and temporarily resist further field coupling**? Tied to
  **field rigidity**" (Concept 20 future-expansion, DoA conv. L4280) and "Is there a **critical RD value beyond which
  additional gradient no longer increases force** (analogous to **elastic yield points**)?" (Concept 20 audit, DoA conv. L7242);
- **tensorial / asymmetric / non-universal** — "Force exertion may vary directionally and **asymmetrically**, reflecting
  the **tensorial nature of their coupling**, modulated by local Manifold-Topology curvature and **Payan-State (mis)alignment**"
  (DoA conv. L7175);
- **finite-range / boundary-shaped** — "Boundary-Induced Forces … reflection, absorption, or holographic projection"
  (DoA conv. L7206);
- plus the May-17 **manifold load-capacity** picture: a "resonant load capacity" that, when exceeded, **collapses/disperses**
  (D0517 §6).

**Current result.** The Gravity-D spatial effective-medium campaign (catalog §8A) measured a real force and then
*rejected it for failing Newtonian benchmarks*:
- **SUPPORTED:** the force is exactly **gradient-energy weighted**, `F = -D ∫ ∇N|∇ψ|² dV`, leading term
  `F_cg ≈ -D·K_grad·∇N` (GR-D-1/2).
- **REJECTED FOR THIS MODEL:** no inverse-square exterior (GR-D-3), no shell theorem (GR-D-4), **no universal free fall —
  probe internal structure changes the force by ~54.6%** (GR-D-5), no global point-ray description (GR-D-6).
- The production geometry's load response is a **saturation cliff, not a graded potential** (GR-1 FALSIFIED); de-saturating
  made it *steeper* (GR-2 FALSIFIED). Sector **PAUSED** as "uninterpretable in production geometry."

**The missed connection.** The workflow's implicit validation target was **Newtonian/graded/universal gravity**, and by
that target the results are failures → "paused." But **IRER Concept 20 never predicted Newtonian gravity.** It predicted a
force that is gradient-derived (✓ GR-D-2 confirms exactly this), **saturating/"capping out"** (✓ the saturation cliff),
**probe-/alignment-dependent and non-universal** (✓ the 54.6% probe-structure dependence *is* "asymmetric coupling modulated
by probe structure"), and **finite-range** (✓ short-ranged for compact sources). Point for point, the "REJECTED-for-being-
non-Newtonian" list is a **match to IRER's own predicted force structure.** The saturation cliff Jake's re-entry example
flagged is one facet of a larger pattern: *the whole Gravity-D result set reads as IRER-consistent and Newton-inconsistent
at the same time.*

**Implication (actionable, posture-preserving).** This does **not** upgrade to a gravity claim. It says the **re-entry
target may be miscalibrated**: the current re-entry gate (catalog §8; `IRER_GRAVITY_REENTRY_AUDIT…`) demands a
"stable-overdense-load-on-ρ_vac-background" that behaves *graded/Newtonian*, i.e. it asks IRER to reproduce a theory it
never was. A more faithful test asks: **does the measured finite-width, saturating, probe-dependent force match IRER's
*specific* predicted force structure** — gradient-derived **and** Payan/alignment-modulated **and** saturating at a critical
RD? Concretely: (a) treat the saturation cliff as the candidate "field-rigidity / RD yield-point" *observable* and
characterize its critical-RD onset rather than trying to remove it; (b) test whether the 54.6% probe-dependence tracks
**Payan/phase alignment** between probe and source (Concept-20 predicts asymmetric coupling is *alignment*-modulated — see
MC-3), which would convert an apparent "non-universality bug" into a predicted signal. Both are within the existing
Gravity-D mirror; neither reopens production gravity.

## MC-2 — Stability ⟂ transport *is* "Dynamic Informational Inertia" (Jake's proposed mass mechanism)  [strong]

**Recovered concept.** "**Dynamic Informational Inertia (DII):** a resistance to reconfiguration of high-coherence quantule
structures — **explains mass-like stability and persistent identity**" (DoA Appendix-B mechanisms list, conv. L447); and
"**resonant inertia** … stable Payan configurations resist perturbation until a critical threshold" (Concept 19, DoA conv.).
The design principle: coherence-stability *is* resistance-to-motion.

**Current result.** The catalog's sharpest empirical dichotomy: **stability and transport are incompatible in the
dissipative sector** — a\* is a "fundamentally stationary dissipative attractor," a kick imparts no net motion
(C-2 FALSIFIED, `L_k=−Dk²−η` real → **no inertial channel**), a static well causes accretion not migration (C-3). Yet the
**conservative** substrates transport cleanly: NLS solitons at v=2Dk (C2.7), and the **KG Q-ball transports inertially
because a 2nd-order substrate gives it configurational inertia — "momentum in ψ_t, not phase"** (C3-1/C3-5).

**The missed connection.** DII was Jake's proposed mechanism for **mass/persistent identity = resistance to
reconfiguration.** The workflow found, empirically and without linking it to DII, that the maximally-coherent stable
structure (a\*) is *maximally resistant to reconfiguration* — it will not move, only accrete. That is DII realized: the
stable attractor's "mass-like stability" *is* its immobility. And the reason transport becomes available in the
conservative KG substrate — **configurational inertia carried in ψ_t** (C3-1) — is a concrete realization of "resonant
inertia" as a genuine inertial degree of freedom rather than a pinning one. So the stability⟂transport incompatibility is
not a surprising obstacle; it is what a DII/resonant-inertia theory predicts: **you cannot have both mass-like pinning and
mobility in the *same* structure — mobility needs the inertia to live in a dynamical (2nd-order) channel, not in the
coherence that pins.**

**Implication.** Gives the empirical dichotomy a theoretical home and a name from the provenance, and sharpens the
transport story: the operative variable separating "pins" from "moves" is *where the inertia lives* (dissipative coherence
vs KG ψ_t) — exactly the DII distinction. Feeds the concept↔implementation register (task #7) as a **CONFIRMED-analogue**
row: DII/resonant-inertia ↔ C-2/C3-1.

## MC-3 — The π/2 phase-force crossover *is* Payan/parallel-alignment governing coupling  [moderate]

**Recovered concept.** Alignment governs coupling and defect formation: "the **alignment or misalignment of Payan States**
… **governs informational coherence** and the formation of Angular Deficits" (Concept 19); "**coupled rotational information
indifferences** … along their **axis of least effort**, within the **informational parralel**" (phase-field discussion,
D0518-PF §1); quantule **chiral pairs** couple by relative handedness/phase to build larger structures (D0518-PF §4).

**Current result.** The robust two-body law across *both* conservative substrates: a **π/2 phase-force crossover** — pairs
**attract when Δφ < π/2, repel when Δφ > π/2**, crossover exactly at π/2 (C2.9, C3-7), and this is **cross-substrate
universal** (NLS ≡ KG). Relative phase Δφ is the control variable.

**The missed connection.** The workflow reports the π/2 law as an abstract phase-dependent force. But **relative phase Δφ
between two coherent cores is precisely the "Payan/parallel alignment" variable** of the recovered theory — the degree to
which two rotational/spin-carrying structures are aligned along their informational parallel. IRER predicted, in 2025, that
**alignment governs whether structures cohere (attract/bind) or defect (repel)**; the 2026 result is that alignment (Δφ)
governs attraction vs repulsion with a sharp coherence/decoherence boundary at π/2. The universal π/2 boundary is a
candidate realization of the coherence↔angular-deficit threshold. This connection is currently *unmade* — the two-body law
has no theoretical label in the results docs; the recovered concept supplies one.

**Implication.** (a) Reframes π/2 as the **coherence-alignment threshold** predicted by the Payan/parallel concept, giving
the "chiral pairs build larger structures" idea (D0518-PF §4) an empirical anchor: capture/binding above alignment,
defect/repulsion below. (b) Directly feeds MC-1's test: if inter-structure coupling is Payan-alignment-modulated, the
gravity probe-dependence (54.6%) should track probe↔source Δφ. (c) A cheap confirming test already exists in-harness —
correlate the C2.9/C3-7 force sign with a Payan/∇φ-alignment readout of the pair.

## MC-4 — The exact-anti-phase pass-through node *is* the "destructive-interference / Mirrored-Collapse" channel  [moderate]

**Recovered concept.** Two recovered items: (i) Jake's **origin conjecture** that structure forms where far-reaching waves
resonate and "all that potentiality tries to collapse on a central spot" while "the wave function tries to reach the
average" — a **destructive-interference-node** intuition (D0518-PF §11); (ii) **Mirrored Collapse Divergence**: entangled
"OIWs **fail to resolve to a single event and instead mirror each other across an Informational Parallel**" (SPTT text,
DoA Part 3 / D0518-PF §18).

**Current result.** Transmission (pass-through) between two coherent cores occurs **only at exact anti-phase Δφ = π**, a
"**destructive-interference NODE feature**": cores reach the midplane node (sep_min ≈ 0.1) and **re-emerge coherently**
(≤7% radiation) instead of capturing — everywhere else they capture/merge (C3-10, C2.10b, cross-substrate). The static π/2
force does *not* govern this; the node is a distinct channel.

**The missed connection.** The one regime where two structures **fail to co-resolve/merge and instead pass through
mirrored across the interaction axis** is exactly Jake's "Mirrored Collapse Divergence" description, and the mechanism —
a **destructive-interference node** at the meeting point — is his origin-story matter/wave-cancellation intuition. The
workflow found the anti-phase transmission node as a clean but *un-interpreted* substrate feature; the recovered theory
names it and predicts precisely its character (fail-to-resolve + mirror across the parallel, no merge).

**Implication.** Candidate identification: **anti-phase pass-through node ↔ Mirrored Collapse Divergence**. If pursued,
the theory predicts the pass-through pair should show *mirrored* (anti-correlated) internal phase across the node — a
concrete observable to add to the collision harness. [speculative on the entanglement framing; strong on the
destructive-interference-node identification.]

## MC-5 — The FMIA "low-resistance channel" (routing) was nulled only where it *couldn't* work  [moderate → a gap, not a refutation]

**Recovered concept.** FMIA / Informational Parallels: systems move along "**manifoldic lines**" — **low-resistance
coherent channels** of minimal informational dissipation (v9 Concept 21; phase-field §1). Later refinement: "**FMIA is
co-evolved, not static**" and "splash **carves/reinforces FMIA pathways**" (DoA Part 3 change-log) — i.e. channels form
*dynamically* under activity.

**Current result.** Every routing/channel predictor came back **NULL** — tensor-routing NO_SUPPORT (C-6), Payan-routing
NO_SIGNAL (C-7), bridge-ratio NULL (C-9) — **but all in the static Phase-C dissipative sector**, where the attractor doesn't
move at all. Meanwhile D4 **CONFIRMED** a real short-range density/geometry coupling channel (conductance ∝ 1/spacing), and
TG-4 explicitly proposes "**FMIA bridges emerge as low-resistance decay routes under *dynamic* temporal-geometric stress**"
as **OPEN**.

**The missed connection / gap.** The FMIA-channel hypothesis was tested **only** in the regime the rest of the catalog shows
is transport-dead (static, pinned, dissipative) and against **static geometric proxies** — and the recovered concept says
FMIA channels are **co-evolved/dynamic** ("carved by activity"), i.e. they should *not* exist as pre-baked static bridges.
So the routing NULLs may have **falsified the wrong version** of the concept (static channels in a static sector), leaving
the actual claim — *dynamically-carved low-resistance routes in a sector that transports* — **untested**. D4's confirmed
coupling channel and the conservative transport sector are where it could actually show up.

**Implication.** A gap the nulls may have prematurely closed. Candidate test: look for **activity-carved low-resistance
routes in the conservative transport substrate** (NLS/KG), or under TG dynamic stress (TG-4), rather than static geometric
bridges in Phase C. Mark the routing concept in the register as **NULL-in-static-sector, UNTESTED-in-transport-sector**,
not globally NULL.
