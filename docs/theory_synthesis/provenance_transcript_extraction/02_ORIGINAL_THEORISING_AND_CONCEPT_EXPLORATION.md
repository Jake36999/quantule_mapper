# Original Theorising and Concept Exploration

This document gathers raw IRER concept exploration: first-principles reasoning, early terminology, speculative
branches, and concept seeds. It is intentionally broader than current Quantule Mapper evidence.

## 1. Project Origin: Testing an Intuitive Physics Picture

Primary source: `Aleheia'sChat.txt`, line 95.

The root bridge transcript records that the project grew from the author's attempt to understand whether his way of
thinking about physics was correct. The author describes early interest in matter manipulation, matter "down to an
atomic level", gravity, magnetism, nuclear forces, quantum counterintuitiveness, rotational energy conservation,
gyroscopic precession, and centripetal force.

Classification: `original_theorising`, `non_physics_context`.

Consolidated meaning:

- IRER did not begin as a request for AI to invent a theory.
- It began as a long-running effort to reconcile intuition about matter, fields, force, resonance, and conservation
  with known physics.
- This origin explains why early IRER language often moves through wave functions, energy density, frequencies,
  resonance, and informational structure before settling into formal terms.

## 3. AIS/PIF and Informational Indifference

Sources:

- Declaration v9, AIS and Informational Indifference sections.
- `F:\transcripts\2025_05_May\20250518_091613_Phase-field Informational Resonance.txt`, lines 6-40.
- `F:\transcripts\2025_05_May\20250517_135856_IRER Fundamental Forces Development.txt`.

Original theorising:

- The substrate is a pre-spacetime or non-temporal field of potential.
- "Informational Indifference" is explored as the tendency of potentiality or informational states toward equilibrium.
- Deviations from indifference create gradients, and gradients drive change.
- The author explicitly toys with "potentiality indifference" as perhaps a better wording.

Early error/risk:

- The origin of the first gradient is not explained.

  minimization, or attractor dynamics.

Current consolidation:

- Keep the IRER term as authorial terminology.
- Translate it to standard readers as a variational/equilibrium-seeking principle.

## 5. Quantules, Payan States, and Angular Deficits

Primary sources:

- `F:\transcripts\2025_05_May\20250518_224554_Quantules and Payan States.txt`.
- `F:\transcripts\2025_05_May\20250518_091613_Phase-field Informational Resonance.txt`, lines 74-99 and 789-849.
- Declaration v9 Appendix A.

Original exploration:

- A Quantule is explored as a coherent resonance cluster or irreducible informational unit.
- Payan states are explored as "spin along" an axis of a related dimension or manifoldic line.
- Angular deficits are explored as incomplete phase closure in intersecting OIWs.
- Spin is proposed as a compensatory rotational response to the missing/incomplete angle.

Concept branch:

- Type I Quantules: self-contained deficit/spin.
- Type II Quantules: deficit offloaded to the field/manifold.
- Type III Quantules: hybrid or fractional modes.

Early errors/risks:

- Mapping Type I/II/III directly to fermions/bosons/anyons is attractive but unsupported without exchange-statistics
  derivations.
- Angular deficit needs a bookkeeping identity: the deficit must be conserved or accounted for if it creates spin.
- Later Phase C tests did not validate Payan/routing predictors.

Consolidated meaning:

- Keep Payan and Angular Deficit as original conceptual mechanisms.
- Treat them as speculative unless a later implementation explicitly measures them.
