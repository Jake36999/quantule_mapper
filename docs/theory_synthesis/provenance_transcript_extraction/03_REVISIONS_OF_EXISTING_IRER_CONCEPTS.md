# Revisions of Existing IRER Concepts

This document records refinements, corrections, renamed concepts, contradictions, and superseded interpretations.
It preserves mistakes as part of concept development.

## 1. From Intuitive Physics to Cautious Formal Translation

Early IRER language used terms such as "desire", "wants equilibrium", "pulls", and "chooses". Later documents should
translate these into:

- relaxation toward lower effective potential;
- attractor basin selection;
- gradient-driven dynamics;
- variational path bias;
- stability under coherence constraints.

Revision status: `revised`.

Reason:

- The theory's intent is mechanistic, not agentic.
- This keeps original authorial terminology while making the physics-facing expression safer.

## 2. Informational Indifference

Original exploration:

- Informational Indifference as the substrate's tendency toward equilibrium.
- Possible renaming to "Potentiality Indifference" appears in the May phase-field thread.

Revision:

- Preserve "Informational Indifference" as canonical IRER terminology.
- Add "potentiality indifference" as a semantic clarification, not a replacement.
- Explain to standard readers as an equilibrium/variational principle.

Open tension:

- The origin of the first deviation from perfect indifference remains unresolved.

Status: `revised`, `open`.

## 3. PAS and Probability Language

Original exploration:

- PAS can sound like "actualization probability" or likelihood.

Revision:

- Current framing should state that PAS is a deterministic readiness/activation landscape unless a model explicitly
  adds stochastic dynamics.
- Probability is at most emergent/coarse-grained from unresolved complexity.

Status: `revised`.

Relevant later check:

- Existing theory synthesis warns not to read PAS/RFD as stochastic quantum collapse.

## 4. RFD and Collapse

Original exploration:

- Collapse as resolution from potential to actual.
- Collapse duality as stabilization versus novelty generation.
- Splash as redistribution of collapse effects.

Revision:

- Collapse should be described as continuous nonlinear field relaxation or thresholded event dynamics in model
  analogues.
- Observer-triggered quantum collapse language should be avoided unless explicitly contrasting with Copenhagen.

Status: `revised`.

Open tension:

- A full first-principles derivation of RFD remains separate from implementation analogues.

## 5. Quantules

Original exploration:

- Quantules were described as fundamental informational units or coherent resonance clusters.
- Later they were linked to angular deficit and Payan-state compensation.

Revision:

- For current public-facing theory, define Quantules broadly as localized coherent resonance packets.
- Keep "physical particles are Quantules" as speculative, not confirmed.
- In Quantule Mapper, treat solitons, Q-balls, and attractor nodes as analogues, not proof of particles.

Status: `revised`.

## 6. Payan States and Angular Deficits

Original exploration:

- Payan states as spin along a manifold axis.
- Angular deficit as missing phase closure causing spin.
- Quantule types classified by how deficit is retained or offloaded.

Revisions and known problems:

- A direct mapping to known particle spin/statistics is unsupported without derivation.
- A deficit-to-spin mechanism needs a conservation/accounting identity.
- Phase C tests did not support Payan/routing predictors.
- The concept remains useful as a hypothesis for internal modes, phase/chirality, or topological defect behavior.

Status: `speculative`, `partially revised`, `partly null-tested`.

## 8. Gravity-Like Mechanisms

Original exploration:

- Gravity as density/geometry gradient.
- Dense neutron-like quantules as manifold load regulators.
- Supernova/black-hole analogies as overload or topology failure.
- Far-field mapping as a later August/September bridge.

Revision:

- Replace "gravity" with "gravity-like", "geometry-density interaction", or "effective-medium response" unless
  citing a historical transcript.
- Do not claim Newtonian gravity, universal free fall, geodesic validation, or matter-source confirmation.
- Current gravity-like branch is split between conceptual IRER source semantics and tested spatial effective-medium
  response.

Status: `revised`, `partially implemented by analogue`, `not gravity proof`.

## 11. Overclaiming Corrections

Several transcript passages, especially October/November presentation and validation material, use language such as
"decisive validation", "validated framework", "formal proof", or "confirmed physical theory". For the consolidated
theory archive:

- preserve the passages as historical development and communication artifacts;
- mark them as `overstrong_historical_language`;
- restate current position with modern caution;
- do not delete them, because the point of this archive is also to show errors and evolving discipline.

Status: `superseded_language`.
