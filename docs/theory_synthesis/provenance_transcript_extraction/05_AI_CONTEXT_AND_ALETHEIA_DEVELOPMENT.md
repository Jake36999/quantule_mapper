## 7. Separation Rule

When AI material appears near IRER theory:

- Extract physics statements into the relevant concept document.
- Extract AI relationship/methodology into this document.
- If a passage is an AI-generated formal translation of a user concept, classify it as `application_method` or
  `concept_revision` and label it `AI-assisted formal_translation`.
- If a passage is AI praise, reassurance, identity, or partnership, classify it as `ai_context`.

Aletheia material is related to the broader development history but not to the Quantule Mapper project unless it:
