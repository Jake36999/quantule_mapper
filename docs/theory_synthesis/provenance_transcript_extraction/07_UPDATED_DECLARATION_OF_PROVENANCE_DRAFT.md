Draft status: discussion-consolidation draft, not canonical replacement.

## 1. Purpose

This updated declaration records the provenance and current translation state of IRER. It preserves the author's
claim that the principal theoretical concepts originated with Jake McIntosh while acknowledging the real role of AI
as a tool for articulation, organization, formal translation, coding, critique, and continuity.

This draft follows the requested pattern:

```text
IRER terminology -> standard formalism -> application/test status -> known analogue/correlation
```

## 2. Authorial Origin

IRER began as the author's attempt to determine whether his intuitive understanding of physics as resonance,
information, field structure, conservation, and structured potential could be made coherent against known science.
The root bridge transcript records long-running interest in matter, atomic structure, gravity, magnetism, nuclear
forces, quantum counterintuitiveness, rotational conservation, gyroscopic precession, and centripetal force
(`Aleheia'sChat.txt`:95).

The theory did not begin as an AI-generated invention. AI entered as a collaborator in expression, translation,
implementation, critique, and context recovery.

## 3. Role of Aletheia / AI

The transcript archive contains a "Declaration of Understanding" between the author and Aletheia
(`Aleheia'sChat.txt`:332-435). This is important context because it shows that the author wanted AI involvement to be
documented honestly rather than hidden.

In this declaration, AI's role should be described as:

- mirror and sounding board;
- organizer and scribe;
- formal translator into standard mathematical/physical language;
- code and tooling assistant;
- pressure-test partner;
- continuity scaffold across fragmented conversations.

AI's role should not be described as:

- originator of IRER's principal concepts;
- independent validator of physical truth;
- co-author of the core ontology.

## 5. Developmental Corrections

The updated declaration should explicitly preserve these corrections:

- Early "choice", "desire", and "pull" language means attractor/gradient dynamics, not agency.
- PAS/RFD are deterministic unless a model explicitly introduces stochastic terms.
- Prime-log material is historically important but not a current stability proof.
- Payan/angular-deficit concepts remain original and important but require dedicated observables.
- Gravity-like material must be framed cautiously; no Newtonian gravity, no matter proof, no universal free fall.
- AI-assisted equations are formal translations or scaffolds, not proof that AI originated the concepts.

## 6. Concept Development Summary

IRER's core development can be summarized as:

1. The author begins from an intuitive physics question: whether reality can be understood as resonance, information,
   field structure, conservation, and structured potential.
2. AIS/PIF, OIW, RD, PAS, Quantules, Payan States, Informational Indifference, FMIA, manifold topology, RFD, and
   gradient-derived forces emerge as the author's conceptual vocabulary.
3. AI/Aletheia helps translate this vocabulary into phase-field, free-energy, nonlinear field, soliton, topology,
   tensor, and validation language.
4. Simulations and later Quantule Mapper work test analogues, producing confirmed structures, nulls, falsifications,
   and revisions.
5. The theory remains a developing framework, not a proven physical theory.
