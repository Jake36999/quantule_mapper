## Trace

### 1. Claimed pre-AI conceptual origin, circa 2022-2024

The declaration asserts that Jake developed AIS as a pre-spacetime informational substrate before substantive AI
engagement. It also states that Informational Indifference creates gradients and that gradient dynamics drive subsequent
interactions. For FMIA, the declaration places "axis of least effort" and "informational parralel" in Jake's core IRER
dynamics before AI formal naming.

This is a provenance claim, not an independent archival proof. The next evidence pass should locate the cited
`transcript.txt` and `sctriptpt2.txt` snippets if they are available.

### 2. May 17 2025: forces and paths are explored through PIF, gradients, and entropy

The May corpus includes earlier AIS/PIF references on May 14, but the May 17 force-development transcript is the earliest
located direct source in this pass for the path/least-activity branch. It frames forces as emergent from resonance
interactions within PIF and links gravity-like behavior to informational density gradients. Jake then moves
into manifold angles, probability parallels, entropy reduction, delocalization, spin/conservation, and "path of least
activity."

The reasoning sequence is:

1. Forces need not be primitive; they may emerge from resonance and field topology.
2. Stable paths may be selected because they reduce entropy or delocalization.
3. "Path of least activity" gives a minimization intuition before the more polished FMIA term.
4. Dense-state/load speculation then branches outward into matter and astrophysical analogies.

The dense-state branch is retained as history but marked speculative.

### 3. May 18 2025: phase-field discussion becomes the first located formal translation

The May 18 phase-field transcript is central. Jake asks whether a phase-field approach could be useful, then supplies the key original
vocabulary: informational/potentiality indifference, equilibrium, axes of least effort, informational parallels, and
manifoldic lines. The AI translates those ideas into:

- phase-field order parameters;
- free-energy functionals;
- local energy density;
- gradient minimization;
- geodesic/least-action analogies;
- entropy gradients;
- spin torque.

Jake then asks whether this describes forces of an IRER field. The AI's answer gives a formal analogy in terms of negative
gradients and torque-like terms. This is the clearest located division between Jake's original concepts and AI-assisted
standard-formalism translation.

### 4. Root bridge transcript: concepts are recap-integrated with Aletheia

`Aleheia'sChat.txt` contains mixed IRER, Aletheia, and AI-continuity material. For this family, it is useful but secondary.
It shows the concepts being recapped together: AIS/PIF, OIWs, gradients, and FMIA as least-resistance paths. It also
contains a valuable caution: FMIA is described as only partially matched by simulations and not yet a solved variational
problem.

It also contains poetic language where the substrate "desires" indifference. This is relevant to vocabulary drift and
project genesis, but it should not be used as physics evidence.

### 5. August/September 2025: ontology consolidation

By late August and early September, summaries and book maps treat AIS/PIF, Informational Indifference, and FMIA as core
IRER first principles. This indicates consolidation, not first appearance. The "process, not stuff" phrasing is useful as
an ontology label but may be AI editorial language unless earlier Jake wording is found.

### 6. November 2025: formalism gap becomes explicit

The November fallback source introduces a major corrective note: S-NCGL is described in report language as useful as a
computational engine or analogue, but relying on imported mathematics is the project's major formal risk. The desired next
step is deriving the equation from AIS and Informational Indifference using a Lagrangian framework.

This matters for the updated declaration: phase-field/S-NCGL formalism should be described as AI-assisted formal mapping
and computational analogue unless and until derivation from IRER axioms is documented.

### 7. December 2025: FMIA proxy is superseded in implementation

The December post-mortem states that the worker was still operating on an obsolete FMIA Stable Proxy while the hunter had
moved to S-NCGL parameters. The correction was to replace the old worker with S-NCGL-native logic.

This is a crucial revision. FMIA remains a conceptual principle and target semantics, but the "FMIA Stable Proxy" should
not be presented as the current Quantule Mapper physics engine.
