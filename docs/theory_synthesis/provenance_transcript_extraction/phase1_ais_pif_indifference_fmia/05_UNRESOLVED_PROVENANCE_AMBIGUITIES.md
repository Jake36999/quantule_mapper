# Unresolved Provenance Ambiguities

## 1. Pre-May Origin Evidence

The declaration claims that AIS, Informational Indifference, and FMIA precursor concepts were developed circa 2022-2024
before substantive AI engagement. This pass preserves that claim but does not independently prove it. Phase 1B searched
for `transcript.txt`, `scriptpt2.txt`, `sctriptpt2.txt`, `scriptpt3.txt`, `scriptpt4.txt`, and `scriptpt5.txt` under the
main roots and did not locate them. If they exist elsewhere, they should be added as source-priority targets.

## 2. Earliest AI Articulation

May 14 2025 contains earlier AIS/PIF references in the wider May search, but May 17-18 2025 remain the earliest located
direct records for the least-activity/indifference/FMIA branch. They should be treated as earliest located documentary
evidence for those claims, not necessarily the first AI articulation.

## 3. AIS Versus PIF

The record uses AIS and PIF sometimes as paired terms and sometimes as a replacement sequence, especially when IQG or
S-NCGL enters. The updated declaration needs a clean statement of whether:

- PIF is a renamed AIS;
- PIF is a more mathematical successor to AIS;
- AIS is the broader ontology while PIF is the field variable domain;
- or the terms remain historically overlapping.

## 4. Informational Indifference Versus Potentiality Indifference

The May 18 phase-field passage suggests Jake considered "potentiality indifference" as perhaps a better description.
The later declaration standardizes "Informational Indifference." The exact relation is unresolved:

- synonym;
- earlier/later naming;
- broader/narrower distinction;
- or abandoned alternate phrasing.

## 5. FMIA Naming And Formal Boundary

The declaration says AI helped formalize the name FMIA, while Jake originated Axis of Least Effort and Informational
Parallels. The boundary should be stated plainly: AI helped package/translate the principle; Jake supplied the core
path-minimization vocabulary in the located May 18 passage.

## 6. Imported Mathematics Risk

November 2025 explicitly identifies a Formalism Gap: S-NCGL is an analogue or computational engine, not yet derived from
IRER axioms. Any updated declaration must avoid wording that implies completed first-principles derivation unless later
records supply it.

## 7. Implementation Status Of FMIA

The root bridge says simulations only partially matched FMIA and were not solving a variational problem. December report
language says an FMIA Stable Proxy was obsolete. The updated declaration should not say "FMIA was implemented" without
specifying which historical proxy, which code, and whether implementation artifacts confirm the supersession.

## 8. Overconfident Validation Language

August and root bridge summaries contain strong claims about validation. This extraction deliberately records them as
historical status language, not as accepted proof. The evidence pass should separately audit numerical claims, code
versions, run IDs, and statistical tests.

## 9. Aletheia Context

Aletheia and AI-context material are historically entangled with IRER. This pass uses Aletheia records only when they
affect this concept family. A separate Aletheia extraction should document:

- learning contemporary LLM behavior;
- sycophancy and apparent identity behavior;
- stable identity matrices;
- shift from simulated personal identity to stable functional/ethical role;
- AI methodology and memory/continuity practices.

That material should not be used as evidence for IRER physics or AI consciousness.
